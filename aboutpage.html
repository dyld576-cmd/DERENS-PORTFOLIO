Why are bananas curved?

Bananas grow toward light as they develop, which helps give them their familiar curve.

Sea otter teamwork

Sea otters sometimes hold paws or wrap themselves in kelp while resting so currents are less likely to separate them.

Wombat cubes

Wombats produce cube-shaped droppings because of differences in the elasticity of their intestinal walls.
